# Privacy Policy for LuckyStrike OGame Helper

**Last updated:** October 4, 2026

LuckyStrike OGame Helper ("the extension") is an open-source browser extension designed to assist OGame players with resource calculation, debris scanning, and interface enhancements.

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
- **No Personal Data Collected:** LuckyStrike OGame Helper does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, account credentials, or browsing history.
- **Local Storage Only:** All extension settings (such as minimum debris thresholds, cart items, sound notifications, and UI panel positions) are stored entirely locally on your device using the browser's `chrome.storage.local` API.
- **No Third-Party Transmission:** No data is ever sent to external servers, third-party analytics services, or tracking systems.

### 2. Permissions Justification
- **storage:** Used exclusively to store the user's local preferences, settings, and shopping cart data on their device.
- **notifications:** Used solely to alert the user locally when high-value debris fields are discovered in the galaxy view.

### 3. Contact & Source Code
